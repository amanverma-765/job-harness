## Job Description

Get hands-on experience building real-world Applied AI solutions using Generative AI, LLMs, React, .NET, and Python. Work alongside experienced engineers and learn how ideas move from prototypes to enterprise-ready solutions. Experiment with emerging AI technologies, solve meaningful problems, and build practical skills that give you a strong start to your engineering career.

## Responsibilities

- Build and enhance applications using React, .NET and/or Python. Strong hands-on development experience.
- Develop UI components, backend services, and REST APIs.
- Experiment with Generative AI, LLMs, and prompt engineering.
- Work with SQL/databases and integrate AI or third-party APIs.
- Write, test, and debug code while learning modern engineering practices.
- Collaborate with the team to turn ideas and prototypes into working solutions.

## Qualifications

- Pursuing BE/BTech/BCA/MCA or equivalent in Computer Science, IT, AI, or a related field.
- Good programming fundamentals with hands-on knowledge of Python and/or C#/.NET.
- Basic knowledge of React, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, and CSS.
- Familiarity with APIs, SQL, Git, and object-oriented programming.
- Basic understanding of Generative AI/LLMs; exposure to RAG or AI agents is a plus.
- Academic, personal, or hackathon projects demonstrating hands-on coding ability.
- Strong problem-solving skills, curiosity, and enthusiasm for learning new technologies.
