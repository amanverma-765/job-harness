# Forward Deployed Engineer Intern - Bynry (Job ID: 430841)

Source: PyjamaHR / Bynry careers portal
Location: Pune, Maharashtra, India (Hybrid)
Employment Type: Internship (6 months, extendable to 9)
Compensation: INR 10,000 / month + 1 paid leave per month
Experience Level: Entry level (0 - 1 years)

## Company Overview

Bynry is a B2B cloud native SaaS company specializing in utility operations and
management. Its flagship product, SMART360 Next, helps small and mid-sized utilities
modernize operations through AI-powered solutions. Bynry describes itself as an
AI-first organization where engineers use intelligent tools to build, test and ship
faster.

## Position Summary

As a Forward Deployed Engineer Intern you are embedded with the engineering team,
working alongside a mentor and with QA, DevOps and Customer Success. You listen to how
utilities actually use SMART360, build the small integration, automation or AI workflow
that solves a real problem, test it, and see it run in a real or customer-like
environment. Explicitly not a sales-demo, support-ticket or QA-only role.

Duration: 6-month internship with immediate start. Extended by 3 months (up to 9 months)
when full-time conversion is evaluated. Conversion is not automatic.

## Responsibilities

### Customer Engagement & Discovery
- Observe customer discovery calls and workshops; take notes, ask clarifying questions,
  summarise requirements.
- Onboard customer feedback to product and engineering; help turn repeated needs into
  reusable components.

### Build & Integrate
- Build small APIs, integrations (REST, webhooks) and automation workflows in Python,
  with mentor review.
- Use SQL to query, check and move data for customer solutions.
- Prototype AI-powered features using LLM APIs and basic retrieval (RAG) workflows, and
  check the outputs for accuracy.

### Testing & Quality
- Own end-to-end testing: functional, integration, regression and UAT.
- Identify, troubleshoot and resolve functional issues, configuration gaps and
  client-specific challenges independently.
- Drive quality improvements through test automation, production validation and
  continuous feedback.

### Deployment & Support
- Support deployments using Git, CI/CD and cloud basics (AWS).
- Help monitor, debug and find the root cause of issues.

### Documentation & Reuse
- Document what you build: how it works, how to run it, how a customer team uses it.
- Present demos of your work and contribute reusable components.

Interns are not expected to lead customer workshops, own production systems or carry
on-call duties alone. The mentor stays accountable while ownership grows.

## What You'll Achieve in 6 Months
- Ship at least one small solution (integration, automation or AI workflow) running in a
  real or customer-like environment.
- Back the work with automated tests that pass regression checks.
- Explain a customer problem, the solution and trade-offs in plain language.
- Document the work so someone else can run and maintain it.

- Interest in an engineering career within a SaaS/tech-driven environment.
- Relevant experience (6 months to 1 year) highly appreciated.
