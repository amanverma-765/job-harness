## About Bornwest

Bornwest builds products that bring delight, efficiency, and connection to organizations worldwide. We are a remote-first, high-ownership team that moves fast, values craftsmanship, and treats every engineer as a first-class citizen of the product. We are growing our India team and looking for passionate fresh graduates who are eager to learn, build, and grow with us.

## The Role

We are hiring Software Developers with 0-2 years of experience to join our India engineering team. This is a full-stack role: you will work on both backend (Ruby on Rails) and frontend (JavaScript / React), building real features for real customers from Day 1.

We are looking for people who are passionate, curious, and self-motivated. You do not need to know everything: you need to love learning, take ownership, and never stop asking questions.

## What You Will Do

- Build and ship features across our product using Ruby on Rails and JavaScript/React
- Write clean, readable, and well-tested code

- Debug and fix issues: own problems end-to-end
