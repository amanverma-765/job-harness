## About Juspay
Juspay is India's leading payments infrastructure company, processing over 300 million transactions daily with an annualized total payment volume surpassing $1 trillion across UPI, credit cards, and digital payment rails. Juspay powers payments for leading enterprises including Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, Uber, Cred, and PhonePe. The company is renowned for deep functional programming, high-reliability distributed systems, and open-source payment orchestration via Hyperswitch.

## Role Responsibilities
- Work on core backend problems pertaining to Juspay's Centers of Excellence.
- Work on framework development and business enablement initiatives across backend, data pipelines, and infrastructure.
- Build resilient backend business logic to handle evolving payment requirements (UPI, PSP integrations, idempotent transaction processing, and authentication).
- Write concise, reliable expressions of complex payment logic leveraging functional programming principles.
- Develop, maintain, and scale API integrations with banks, acquirers, and payment networks.
- Build automated intelligence from transaction telemetry to enable self-healing routing, anomaly detection, and automated reconciliation.
- Optimize multi-datacenter latency, connection pooling, and edge computing reliability.

## Candidate Requirements
- Solid foundations in Data Structures, Algorithms, DBMS, Operating Systems, and Object-Oriented or Functional Programming.
- High proficiency in core backend languages and runtimes (Python, Kotlin/Java, C++, Rust, or Haskell).
- Familiarity with relational databases (PostgreSQL), in-memory caching systems (Redis), and asynchronous queues.
- Passion for solving mission-critical reliability, sub-second latency, and distributed systems bottlenecks.
