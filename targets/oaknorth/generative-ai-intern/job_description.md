## ABOUT US
At OakNorth, we're on a mission to empower ambitious businesses and the communities they serve. Since 2015, we've lent over $21 billion across the UK and US, helped create more than 58,000 new homes and 36,000 new jobs, and supported hundreds of thousands of personal savers, all while driving economic growth in the markets we serve.

## ABOUT THE TEAM
The Management Support team works across OakNorth's leadership to keep strategic and operational work running smoothly. The team handles a wide range of responsibilities, from financial tracking and expense management to business research, and is known for being trusted, accurate, and responsive. Much of the team's current tooling runs on Excel, and there's a real appetite to modernise. This is a team that values clear thinking, strong execution, and people who take ownership of making things better.

## ABOUT THE ROLE
We're looking for an AI Intern to join the Management Support team in Gurgaon. You'll work hands-on to identify where manual, spreadsheet-based processes can be replaced with smarter, AI-powered tools, and then build them. This role is for someone who's genuinely curious about applied AI, comfortable working independently, and motivated by the tangible impact of turning a clunky spreadsheet into something that actually works.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Audit existing Excel-based workflows across the team and identify where automation, AI tools, or improved interfaces would meaningfully reduce manual effort, then design and build those solutions.
- Build and maintain functional tools, dashboards and trackers using Python, VBA, and AI platforms (including Claude, ChatGPT and Notion) that the team can rely on day-to-day.
- Conduct research on internal business questions and external topics (market, industry, or regulatory) and present findings in clear, actionable formats.
- Work closely with team members to understand pain points, translate them into technical requirements, and deliver solutions iteratively with their input.
- Document tools, monitor their usage, and track the time and effort they save, identifying issues early and improving based on real-world use.

## YOU'LL BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU HAVE
- Strong proficiency in Excel and working knowledge of Python and/or VBA, you're comfortable building, not just using.
- Hands-on experience with GenAI tools (Claude, ChatGPT or similar) and an intuition for where they add genuine value versus where they don't.
- Strong written communication and research skills, you can synthesise complex information into something clear and useful.
- A systems mindset: you notice when something is inefficient, think through how to fix it structurally, and follow through independently.

## Nice to Have
- A degree in Computer Science or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Prior experience building tools, dashboards, or interfaces, whether through internships, coursework, or personal projects.
- Familiarity with Notion as a productivity and documentation platform.
