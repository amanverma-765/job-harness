## About Agoda

At Agoda, we bridge the world through travel. Our story began in 2005, when two lifelong friends and
entrepreneurs, driven by their passion for travel, launched Agoda to make it easier for everyone to
explore the world. Today, we are part of Booking Holdings [NASDAQ: BKNG], with a diverse team of over
7,000 people from 90 countries, working together in offices around the globe. Every day, we connect
people to destinations and experiences, with our great deals across our millions of hotels and holiday
properties, flights, and experiences worldwide.

Data and technology are at the heart of our culture, fueling our curiosity and innovation.

## What You'll Get to Do

- **Code Like a Pro:** Contribute to a high-quality codebase and work on scalable, fault-tolerant
  systems and APIs that handle millions of requests.
- **Play with Cool Tech:** Use cutting-edge languages like Scala, Kotlin, ReactJS, and C#, and dive
  into data technologies like MSSQL, PG, Kafka and Spark, all while embracing agile development
  practices.
- **Be a CI/CD Rockstar:** Implement state-of-the-art CI/CD and testing techniques to ensure
  everything runs smoothly with zero downtime.
- **AI/ML:** Gain hands-on experience with state-of-the-art AI tools and frameworks, prototype and
  productionize ML/LLM-driven features.
- **Think Big Picture:** Consider the entire product lifecycle, from defining product requirements
  and choosing the right technology to coding standards, agile development, testing, deployment, and
  ongoing monitoring.

## What You'll Need to Succeed (essential)

- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- Familiarity with the fundamentals of Object-oriented and/or Functional programming.
- A grasp of relational and/or non-relational databases.
- Proficient English communication skills.
- A readiness to learn quickly and adapt to new challenges.

## Bonus Points If You Have

- Practical experience with Scala, Kotlin, Java, JavaScript, ReactJS, or C#.
- Exposure to AI/ML frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, PySpark) or AI/ML-related
