## Raw Posting Text

Join Elip, a YC W25 startup, and build an AI career agent used by thousands of job seekers. It's a 6-month internship with a PPO opportunity, in Gurugram or Bengaluru (on-site or remote), paying Rs 75k a month during the internship and Rs 1.5L a month on conversion.

### Elip's take
You'd build the agents, recommendations and semantic search powering an AI career product used by students and early-career people.

### What you'd do
- Build LLM-powered agents that use tools and remember context
- Match people to jobs through retrieval, ranking and personalisation
- Run semantic search over a large, constantly changing jobs index
- Evaluate and improve LLM output quality
- Ship backend features to production and keep them reliable

### They'll screen for
- Hands-on LLM work with prompting, tool use and structured outputs
- Pick up new tools quickly from their docs

### Good to have
- Built a recommender or search system end to end
- Worked with RAG pipelines or LLM evals
- Have a side project live and used by real people
- Some experience with Docker or cloud deploys

### Skills they want
LLMs and agents, Python backends, SQL and APIs, Embeddings

---

## First 30 / 60 / 90 Days (from the YC posting)

- **Week 1:** first change live. Read real conversations and real matches, find one place where Elip sent the wrong job or gave a weak reason, fix it.
- **By day 30:** own a feature end to end (e.g. the reason written beside every match, or how it remembers what a person said they would never take). Decide, ship, prove with an eval you wrote.
- **By day 60:** own a whole product vertical: the conversation that learns what a person wants, the morning read of millions of job postings, the ranking that decides what gets sent, or the company shortlist. Its quality is the owned number.
- **By day 90:** work forward-deployed, sit with hiring companies and matched candidates, ship the fix that week.
- **At six months:** PPO decision based on what was owned and shipped.

## Who Can Apply
Students and recent graduates based in India, graduating batches 2026 to 2030, all branches. No CGPA cutoff, no backlog restriction.

## Interview Process
1. Application via the Elip link (resume plus screening answers).
2. Technical conversation on Google Meet: something you built, how you would approach matching people to jobs, hands-on LLM / retrieval / backend work. No aptitude test, no group discussion.
3. Founder / HR call: what you want from the internship, working style, start date, location.
